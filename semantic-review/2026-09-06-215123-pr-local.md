# Objective-v2 search scoring, evidence, and publication migration

The change replaces dense-bigram-only ranking with a calibrated, correlation-discounted Enigma objective while retaining a separate fail-closed assessment before any break is published. It binds the scoring constants to reproducible Python, Ruby, and Swift receipts, preserves sealed v1 evidence through additive errata and v2 bundles, and updates the C69 Journal, episodes, captures, and generated-site narrative. Formula constants, model identifiers, source relationships, capture metadata, claim epoch, and generated content align across the scoped change. One active BREAK summary still overstates the final assessment as independent, and the user-visible publication adapters are not directly exercised by the focused tests.

**Watch for:** **confirmed (Medium)** — `HELUT/BREAK_P1030680.md:2598` calls the final verdict “independent” even though ranking and assessment share the attested trigram model; **confirmed (Low)** — changed halt, abort, and banner adapters have helper-level coverage but no production-path regression tests.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Objective-v2 standardizes the attack score against n=72 random controls and discounts the correlated trigram contribution rather than letting dense bigram likelihood dominate candidate ranking. The final publication decision is a separate gate with core criteria and a required trigram score, but it is not statistically independent because both stages consume the same trigram evidence.

The original v1 calibration bundle remains byte-preserved, an erratum identifies it as superseded, and the v2 manifest binds current source hashes and model identifiers to independent Python, Ruby, and Swift receipts. Separately sealed dependent reruns prevent the scoring repair from silently rewriting prior history.

Ep00, ep04 linkage, ep10, the HELUT Journal, and the generated companion projection now agree on claim epoch `2026-08-20 / C69`. Capture-local receipts match sealed sources, manifests match tape and MP4 properties, and generated episode projections match canonical JSON; the phrase “independent verdict” in the active BREAK summary is the remaining narrative contradiction.

Focused tests cover calibration, the dense-bigram overfit regression, known-shell recovery, and helper-level fail-closed behavior. They do not traverse the changed CLI, hybrid halt, discriminator banner, or Ostwald abort paths, so a publication-wiring regression could pass the current suite.

The behavioral boundary is:

```text
ciphertext candidates
        |
        v
calibrated attack score + discounted trigram ----> search ordering
                                      |
```
